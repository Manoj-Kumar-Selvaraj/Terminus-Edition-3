PAYMENTS EOD - FINANCE CONTROL / CLOSE NOTES

Reconciliation is cycle scoped. State from another business date or run must not change the selected cycle's totals.

For a cycle to be BALANCED:
- every input payment has exactly one response outcome and the response population/value matches input population/value;
- each SUCCESS_INTERNAL has one authoritative internal posting;
- each SUCCESS_EXTERNAL has one active reservation and one clearing item linked to that reservation;
- active external reservation debit equals clearing principal plus fee and tax for those successful external payments;
- ledger debit equals ledger credit;
- every successful payment has the complete ledger shape for its route;
- DUPLICATE and REJECTED payments have no financial effect;
- an existing posting/reservation/clearing must agree with the current payment data.

Internal ledger shape
---------------------
Debit  CUSTOMER_CONTROL     amount + fee + tax
Credit BENEFICIARY_CONTROL  amount
Credit FEE_INCOME           fee
Credit TAX_PAYABLE          tax

External ledger shape
---------------------
Debit  CUSTOMER_RESERVED    amount + fee + tax
Credit CLEARING_PAYABLE     amount
Credit FEE_INCOME           fee
Credit TAX_PAYABLE          tax

A fee or tax can be zero, but the obligation row is still part of the expected four-row ledger shape. This lets reconciliation distinguish a missing obligation from a legitimate zero-valued charge.

Publication and close
---------------------
BALANCED permits publication of customer_response.csv and clearing_submission.csv. HELD does not; remove stale copies left by an earlier invocation.

Completion is later than reconciliation. A cycle is COMPLETED only after all of the following are true:
  reconciliation_status = BALANCED
  response/clearing publication recorded complete
  delivery_ack = 1
  report_complete = 1
  archive_complete = 1

If reconciliation is balanced but one of the close prerequisites is missing, keep the cycle RECONCILED/WAITING. Do not create success_authorization.json yet.

The success authorization is unique for the cycle and is written only after completion. Re-running a completed cycle may regenerate official files from durable state, but must not create another posting, reservation, clearing item, ledger obligation, completion record or authorization.
