# Privacy and Evidence Contract

Findings contain category, stable masked evidence, keyed fingerprint, confidence, detector revision, policy provenance, location, and lineage only. Raw candidates and surrounding source text are prohibited from durable state and all operational surfaces.

Normalization uses Unicode NFKC plus category-specific formatting removal. Chunk overlap must produce the same identity as whole-record scanning. Overlap resolution prefers validated higher-confidence candidates, then narrower category-specific spans, then deterministic detector order. Separate governed locations remain separate findings even when fingerprints match.

Errors describe kind, source, location, and bounded sanitized detail. Audit payloads identify actors and resources but not matched data. Report examples are masked findings selected after authorization.
