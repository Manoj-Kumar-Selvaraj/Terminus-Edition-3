# Warehouse inventory cutover equivalence

- Repair the inherited warehouse-movement cutover runtime under `/app/equiv`; do not replace the public executable `/app/equiv/bin/equiv-eval`.
- Preserve `init-db`, `describe-layout`, `identity`, and `run`; also keep the supplied operator workflows `preflight`, `audit`, and `archive` working.
- Every command that accepts `--source PATH` or `--layout PATH` must use the caller-provided path rather than a hard-coded default.
- The default record model is `/app/equiv/config/movement.layout.json`; exact packed-decimal, ODO, REDEFINES, malformed-record boundary, durable-state, reconciliation, publication, CLI, and reject-code semantics are delegated to `/app/equiv/ops/runtime-contract.md`.
- The legacy equivalence values are in `/app/equiv/config/legacy.controls`; all six documented controls must be compared independently, together with the documented zero-tolerance safety controls.
- Validate movement shape, reason/type compatibility, item status/policy, warehouse status/capability, available quantity, and weighted inventory valuation before accepting a movement. Movement-shape authority for grading is functional: quantity must be positive; a `RECEIPT` has no source and requires a destination; an `ISSUE` requires a source and has no destination; a `TRANSFER` requires both source and destination and they must differ; an `ADJUSTMENT` requires a source. The item must exist, and item-policy decisions use the solver-visible `items.active`, `items.max_unit_cost`, and `items.quantity_precision` fields. Every warehouse referenced by a movement must exist and have solver-visible `warehouses.active=1`; there is no separate solver-visible receipt/issue/transfer capability switch beyond that active status. Reason/type compatibility is normative: `PO` and `RETURN` are valid for `RECEIPT`; `SALE` for `ISSUE`; `MOVE` for `TRANSFER`; `COUNT`, `DAMAGE`, and `RETURN` for `ADJUSTMENT`; other combinations are invalid.
- Treat an accepted movement's processed row, all inventory effects/position updates, journal event, and checkpoint advance as one durable transaction; rejected movement durability follows the delegated contract.
- Resume only after the last durable sequence and reject source, layout, or business-date changes that do not match the checkpointed generation identity.
- A current-generation exact movement replay must not reapply inventory effects; cross-generation history is not by itself a current-generation duplicate.
- Reconciliation must block publication when any documented legacy control, transfer-balance safety control, or detailed settlement invariant fails. Detailed settlement requires every accepted movement to have at least one inventory effect, forbids duplicate effects of the same effect kind for one movement, and forbids inventory positions with negative quantity or value below `-0.01`; transfer effects also remain subject to the documented zero-net quantity/value safety rule.
- Publish a verified generation atomically and make a repeated publication of the same verified generation idempotent.
- `preflight` must consume the real source/layout, schema health, catalog, input integrity, historical production baseline, and recovery state rather than treating them as decorative data. An empty active item/warehouse catalog or a recovery state that cannot safely continue is not a passing preflight.
- `audit` must expose replay/recovery, checkpoint, accounting, reconciliation, inventory, quarantine, journal, registry, metric, and settlement health for the generation.
- `archive` must verify reports/publication, export controls and deltas, bind lineage/integrity evidence, write a verified archive, and record the archive event. Retention reporting covers report, publication, audit, and quarantine evidence with positive day horizons, and archiving must not delete or mutate the live report/publication set.
- Inspect `/app/equiv/log/archive` and `/app/equiv/ops` for the inherited cutover evidence before changing behavior.
- Keep the SQLite schema/state and report/publication formats compatible with the solver-visible contracts; do not bypass required constraints in application code.
